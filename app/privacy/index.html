Help

Privacy

The short version: Desi Desktop has no accounts, sets no cookies, runs no ads or analytics, and keeps nothing about you on a server.

What stays in your browser

Your tile choices and order, your metro area, layout and shortcuts are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They never leave your device. You can erase them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Searches

The search box sends what you type straight to Google, the same as searching on google.com. Desi Desktop never sees or stores your searches. Google's privacy policy applies to them. The filter box works only inside your browser.

Location

Desi Desktop never asks for your location. If you pick a metro area for the weather, that choice stays in your browser; the forecasts for all areas are downloaded together, so nobody learns which one you chose.

Hosting

The site is a set of static files served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles standard connection data (such as your IP address) to deliver the pages. Desi Desktop does not receive or keep that data.

Links to other sites

Rows and links open other websites, such as news sources and government pages. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave Desi Desktop.

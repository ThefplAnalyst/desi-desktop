About & Sources

Desi Desktop is a free, fast start page for Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nepali and Sri Lankan people living in the US. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

How we keep it honest

  • We only show headlines from sources we have the right to show. Until a publisher's terms are reviewed, its tile shows “Useful links — not a live feed”.
  • Content refreshes on a schedule, so each tile says when its source last updated. Nothing is called “live”.
  • We never write, rewrite or summarize headlines with AI, and we never copy article text or images. Every row links to the original source.

Kinds of tiles

  • Updates come from official or approved sources on a schedule.
  • Curated tiles, such as cricket fixtures and festivals, are written and checked by hand.
  • Useful links tiles are hand-picked website lists, not feeds.
  • Local tiles, such as your shortcuts and family clocks, are worked out in your browser.

Contact

Report a problem or suggest a source on GitHub Issues. Publishers can ask us there to change or remove anything.
